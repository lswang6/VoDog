# VoDog iOS product

Native SwiftUI client with UIKit bridges, CallKit/PushKit and XcodeGen. The challenge WKWebView handles verification only.

Use Apple HIG, system fonts, SF Symbols, Dynamic Type and native navigation. Preserve authentication, media and business contracts. Native screenshots and interaction checks require an iOS simulator or an explicitly configured iPhone; browser checks do not validate this client.

Configuration and build instructions are in [README](README.md).
